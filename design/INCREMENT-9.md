## Interaction/data decisions

Bottom Spaces is now the social landing page. Clara’s prior personal Space moves to `personal-space`, still reachable through You → Space. Friend route hashes carry friend and note IDs so browser Back/Forward, tab switching and deep links preserve the selected person. Maya is the supplied detailed example; other attached portraits receive bounded sample identities and the same illustrative layout. No compatibility/personality inference is performed: reference scores, descriptions and timelines are sample content.

Friend profiles never read the user’s private check-in, journal or capsule state. Public sample Notes are separate seed data. Connection toggles, read state, notes and replies are local to this tab and reset on reload. Composer copy explicitly states this; saving never sends a message or notification. Copied note text is limited to the displayed note. Top9-dot menus remain inactive. Existing home, diary, voice preview, personal profile, entry-book and capsule flows are retained.

Shared period changes filter the local data rather than claiming a remote query. Search matches supplied sample identities by name or handle; empty results have a clear state. Dialogs trap focus across form controls, make the frame background inert, close on Escape, and restore focus. Dynamic labels, readable item names, aria-current navigation, and keyboard-scrollable long views remain native DOM. Reduced-motion policy is inherited.
